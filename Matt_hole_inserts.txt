Hi Markus,
 
So the M32 punch I have for work is made by PlumBoss (Chassis Punch 1 ¼”(32mm) #CP32 , it’s not the model sold by Sydney tools but the Plumbing supplier had stock whereas Sydney tools was order in. It has a slightly large arbor meaning your pilot hole needs to be drilled out to 19mm but it punches cleanly in the fiberglass and steel to 3.5mm. The bearing built into the punch is far better that the Ruko M35 punch I have at home…  The PlumBoss unit I punched 7 holes in Aluminium in quick succession using the rattle gun, holes all looked burr free.  
 
https://www.plumboss.com.au/products/chassis-punch-1-1-4-inch-32mm-cp32?_pos=1&_psq=cp32&_ss=e&_v=1.0
 
I did look at the 12 piece set, but thought the Hydraulic driver would fail when you need it most and then you’re stuck. Additionally I’m sort of happy with the M32mm Gland functionality, Multi hole inserts and Adapters for other size fittings

### Power sources
* Mains power (Ecosense Forest, Kranzberger Forst)
* Generator Tumbarumba, Wombat Forest, (not recommended, check ICOS note)
* Solar (solar panels on tower can affect the wind flow and radiation patterns.) Consider wind loading on tower!
* DIY battery system / professionally installed power system
* 240V not allowed beyond inverter and power boards.
